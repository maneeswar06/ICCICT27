About the Conference

Where Intelligence Meets Connectivity

The International Conference on Computational Intelligence and Communication Technologies (ICCICT 2K27) brings together researchers, academicians, industry experts and students from across the globe to share cutting-edge innovations and research outcomes.

The conference provides a premier platform for discussing recent advances, emerging trends and real-world applications in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, IoT, Wireless Networks and next-generation communication systems.

Selected papers will be recommended for publication in reputed indexed journals and conference proceedings. Publication and indexing details will be announced officially.

Objectives

  • Bring together researchers, academicians, industry experts and students.
  • Share innovations and research outcomes in computational intelligence and communication technologies.
  • Discuss recent advances, emerging trends and real-world applications.

Research Areas

Conference Tracks

Eight technical tracks covering computational intelligence and modern communication technologies.

01

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Learning-based systems that perceive, reason and make decisions.

  • Deep Learning & Neural Networks
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Computer Vision
02

Computational Intelligence

Nature-inspired and soft-computing approaches to complex problems.

  • Fuzzy Systems
  • Evolutionary Computing
  • Swarm Intelligence
  • Soft Computing
03

Wireless & Mobile Communication

Next-generation wireless technologies and network architectures.

  • 5G / 6G Networks
  • MIMO & Massive MIMO
  • Cognitive Radio
  • Satellite Communication
04

Internet of Things (IoT)

Connected devices, sensing and intelligence at the edge.

  • Smart Cities & Smart Homes
  • Edge & Fog Computing
  • Wireless Sensor Networks
  • Industrial IoT
05

Big Data & Data Science

Extracting knowledge and insight from large-scale data.

  • Data Mining & Analytics
  • Predictive Modelling
  • Cloud Computing
  • Data Visualization
06

Cyber Security & Blockchain

Securing networks, data and distributed digital systems.

  • Network Security
  • Cryptography
  • Blockchain Technology
  • Intrusion Detection
07

Healthcare & Bio-Informatics

Computational methods applied to health and life sciences.

  • Medical Image Processing
  • Smart Healthcare Systems
  • Bio-Signal Processing
  • Disease Prediction
08

Signal & Image Processing

Analysis, interpretation and enhancement of signals and images.

  • Multimedia Processing
  • Pattern Recognition
  • Speech Processing
  • Remote Sensing
